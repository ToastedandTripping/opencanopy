# OpenCanopy — Charter

**Purpose.** An open-source civic tool for British Columbia's forests whose first phase is a public map that renders BC government data faithfully (old growth, logging, fire, species at risk, carbon), and whose founding promises also include logging-company accountability, watershed report cards, and conservation priority analysis, so anyone can see what has happened to the province's forests without taking anyone's word for it.
**For.** The BC public first, as a conservation reference at opencanopy.ca; conservation advocates and local journalists are the named users the accountability work serves. Built and maintained by Lee alone; only Lee can eyeball the live map.
**Done looks like.** (1) The core map is live with every public layer reading correctly at every zoom, honest status states instead of silent blanks, a green fidelity audit suite, and a tile pipeline that rebuilds from government sources without manual intervention. (2) Each of the other founding promises exists once: one watershed report card, one accountability view, and one conservation priority analysis have shipped. (3) The landing story and the carbon calculator never show a number or a picture the underlying data does not support. Growth after all three exist is maintenance, not new obligation.
**Non-goals.** (1) Synthesis layers (fire-age reconciliation, disturbance models) are not part of the open-source base tileset; the base is government-truthful only. (2) It does not expose the full internal catalog: the public surface was deliberately narrowed to 7 layers plus satellite and 4 presets. (3) It does not dramatize: a visual may not outrun or overstate the data (the timeline playhead is render-gated, the story base must not imply pre-1950 logging).
**Phase arc.**
1. Data pipeline: BC open data to PMTiles on R2 with a 7-audit fidelity suite (Mar to Apr 2026). Gate: audits green, v9 tiles deployed.
2. Public map and readiness: layer status states, share cards, CI, a11y and production audits (Jun to Jul 2026). Gate: no FAIL on security or data; a11y FAILs closed.
3. Scroll-story landing and honest timeline: real VRI base, render-gated playhead, rebuilt carbon calculator (Jun to Jul 2026). Gate: no lying visual survives live QA.
4. Hardening and code-refresh: SSOT consolidation, WFS error taxonomy, CORS lock, dead-code sweep (Aug 2026) (here). Gate: prod audit B, layer audit finished.
5. The founding promises beyond the map: accountability views, watershed report cards, conservation priority analysis, alongside the /map improvement program and new BC layers. Each on Lee's go.
**Drift tripwires.** Scope: is a session building a layer or feature that no BC government dataset backs, or that belongs to the "synthesis" tier being folded into the base? Audience: has the map started optimizing for researchers, developers, or funders rather than a member of the public, an advocate, or a local journalist opening opencanopy.ca on a phone? Built-for-the-builder: is the current relay a rendering or pipeline experiment (shader endgame, dolly video) pulled forward ahead of its gate with no public-facing defect it fixes?
