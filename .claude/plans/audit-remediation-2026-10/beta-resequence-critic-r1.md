Docs-only deliverable that fixes the order and the gate for ~22 relays; it adds ten relays (CI, OPS, NUM, MERGE, MAPFIX, T-pre, PIPE, SPEED, BETA, MATRIX) and a Lee-run REBUILD, and defers four (B′, C′, D′, E′) plus M3b.

**Ground truth read.** The grill (`session-d09d2c/.claude/grills/2026-10-22-opencanopy-path-to-beta.md`, confirmed 2026-10-23), DECISIONS.md (all entries incl. the six of 2026-10-23, now committed at `cdd2f00`), CHARTER.md (unedited, confirmed), handoff.md "Owed right now" (13 items), the program plan's contracts C1-C7, relay specs S0/M1a/M1b/X/M2/S2/M3/K/B′/C′/T and dependency graph, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `gh run view 39866175520`, a local `npm audit --audit-level=high`, `scripts/audit-all.ts`, `scripts/pipeline/rebuild.sh`, `deploy-tiles.sh`, `research/map-visual-audit-2026-08-22.md` (P3 row), ROADMAP Parking Lot.

**Conditional dimensions.**
- **X1 Physical safety — does not fire.** Pure software and documents; no hardware, heat, power or physical output anywhere in the plan.
- **X2 Privacy — FIRES [GATING].** Geolocated coordinates in the /map hash (C6, M3b rounding), a public report form with an optional email on Netlify Forms, client and edge error capture (stack traces, URLs), the analytics tracker.
- **X3 Evidence integrity — FIRES [GATING].** The plan asserts repo facts (CI state, audit figures, defect measurements) that child plans will build on, and it defines the mechanism (NUM) by which every public figure is vouched for.
- **X4 Audience/brand/money — fires [ADVISORY].** Public site, beta label copy, known-issues note, later outreach note. No money, legal terms or signature.
- **X5 Concurrency — fires [ADVISORY].** Two lanes, heavy builds on one host (C7), serialized releases (C2), a running exit clock.
- **X6 Operability — fires [ADVISORY].** OPS monitors, form triage, the exit clock, Lee's quarterly rebuild.

- **X8 Dependencies/perf/cost — fires [ADVISORY].** New third-party services on free tiers (error tool, UptimeRobot, Netlify Forms), dev-dependency bumps, three heavy tile builds inside the gate.

### 2. Approach soundness — CONCERN
Re-sequencing the approved program rather than writing a new one is right, and the lane split holds. Three structural problems: (a) **PIPE's shape depends on work scheduled after it.** `rebuild.sh` today runs five vector phases into *one* archive, then prints manual steps (edit `PMTILES_URL`, run `deploy-tiles.sh`, which uploads one file named from the registry). It builds no forest-age raster, no story products, runs no fidelity suite, uploads nothing. PIPE must therefore produce per-layer archives (T-pre's layout), the area-true raster (MAPFIX's method), the C1 versioned-path constant and the fidelity record — yet its build edge is "after P0b" and it sits in G4 beside MAPFIX, with T-pre in G5. Either PIPE comes after both, or MAPFIX and T-pre are specified as PIPE's build-side batches with their own C1 releases. As written, PIPE lands and is immediately stale. It is also under-tiered at Standard for vector + raster + R2 + verify + record. (b) **T-pre splits one build in two.** The 08-22 P3 fix is one tippecanoe build: per-layer archives, forest-age vectors from z10 only, non-public layers out, the z10+ hole flags. T-pre takes two of four and leaves "forest-age vectors from z10" and "non-public layers out" to "the rest of T" — a second full vector rebuild during beta, and both are byte-weight items that serve SPEED directly. T-pre also omits the reader switch ("renderer keyed by tile URL") without which per-layer archives are unreadable; C1 requires that switch "in a deploy of its own with old-link fixtures." (c) **SPEED's profile is weaker than the program's own milestone.** The program's Verification already requires "the first data frame visible within 10 s on Slow 4G" after S1′/S2. SPEED pins "an ordinary phone on 4G" with Slow 4G "recorded beside it for comparison." If the child plan picks Chrome's faster 4G preset, the beta bar passes on a profile the program had already rejected as acceptance.
**Fix.** PIPE build-after: MAPFIX, T-pre (or absorb them as PIPE batches); tier Complex. T-pre takes all four build-side P3 items plus the reader switch; "rest of T" keeps only the E&N mask, big-tree subset and the empty-tile manifest. SPEED names its profile in this plan (Slow 4G per the program milestone, or state why Lee's "4G" means the faster preset and read the numbers back to him).

## Inversion

The rejected alternative is the approved order: Waves 1-6 as written, then the beta. It wins if goal (2) were a precondition (Lee ruled it is not), or if the beta-specific work were small enough to append after Wave 6 without reordering. The second condition is half true and the plan should say so: the resequence keeps Waves 1-3 almost intact (M1a → M1b → M2 → M3a; S0a → S0b → S1-lite → S1′) and its real moves are two — ten relays inserted and four deferred. Its preconditions (SPEED green on M1b alone; Lee content to open on the scroll story) are testable and one of them is a decision already on the table. The plan should name it as the shortest road, if only to show Lee what each "yes" to the longer options costs.
