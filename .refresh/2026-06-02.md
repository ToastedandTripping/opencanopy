## Recommendations beyond this skill's scope (deliberate decisions for Lee)
- **`logging-risk` is a ROADMAP scope violation.** It's a derived "risk proxy" model; ROADMAP says synthesis layers stay *out* of the government-truth base. Gating fixed the crash, but the layer arguably shouldn't live in the open-source base registry at all. Decide: relocate to a separate synthesis layer set, or accept it.
- **`tap-deferrals` is misnamed** (it's VRI 250+, not TAP deferral boundaries; the *real* TAP data is in `conservation-priority`). Renaming breaks saved URLs / localStorage / presets, so it needs a migration shim — a deliberate call, not a silent cleanup.
- **No `ARCHITECTURE.md`.** The ROADMAP points at `state/plans/opencanopy-audit-architecture.md`, which does not exist in-repo. A short architecture doc (layer registry pattern, dual PMTiles/WFS/raster rendering, story-vs-interactive split) would let future refreshes judge drift against a written design instead of idiom.
