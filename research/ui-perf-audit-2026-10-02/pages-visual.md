## Executive summary

## Editorial assessment: does the story earn the scroll?

The open earns it. A canopy photograph, one declarative sentence, then a single red number on black: what exists, what is lost, in two screens, in a serif with authority. The CTA earns its place too ("Now find your own corner of it." is the best line on the site). The middle does not. On a phone the story goes silent for five screens while a counter runs and red spreads with no key; the explanation arrives after the animation. The pictures then outrun the words at every point where they are most persuasive: four times more red than the hectares in the caption; a "before 1950" baseline that the record cannot date and that barely shows; a timeline whose shape is the history of record-keeping, not of logging; a closing flood that paints standing mature forest in the loss colour while the copy says "the full picture is worse", and a closing number whose denominator is wrong. None of it is fabricated data; all of it is rendering and copy. The honest version will be visibly less red. It is also the only version a journalist cannot take apart in ten minutes, which for a project whose whole position is "the government's own data, read faithfully" is the only kind that persuades.

Where a phone reader drops off: (1) 450vh, the start of five silent screens (F-11, F-04); (2) 1100-1300vh, a second silent run through the fire; (3) 1400-1550vh, the forest vanishes and a red flood plays with no text (F-12, F-02); and before all of these, on a slow connection, the first chapter's empty map (F-24).

## Method and limits

- Story: Playwright headless Chromium (SwiftShader), fresh context, production URL, wait for the story canvas and network idle (75 s cap), then scroll in 5vh increments and capture every 50vh with a DOM probe (visible card, card rect, counter text, card/counter overlap): phone 390x844 DPR 3, phone 360x740 DPR 2, desktop 1440x900, and phone 390 with `page.emulateMedia({reducedMotion:"reduce"})`. 36-37 stops each.
- Contrast: per-25vh tight text-rect sampling against a text-hidden screenshot, per-pixel composite including colour alpha and ancestor opacity; median and 5th percentile reported.
- Data checks: R2 tiles and shipped PNGs measured directly in Python (area-weighted Mercator rows); BC WFS hit counts via `resultType=hits`; one proxy read of the calculator path with an Origin header (the same request a visitor's "Visible area" makes).
- External facts: Price, Holt & Daust 2020 (PDF text extracted, saved at data/price-holt-daust-2020.pdf); StatCan Table 15.6; BC Environmental Reporting timber-harvest indicator; Wilderness Committee and Global News pages, URLs inline.
- Not measured: real-device timings (machine load ~24 on 4 cores); Ko-fi pages (Cloudflare challenge); screen-reader behaviour of the year counter (inferred from code); desktop calculator panel (click timed out under CPU starvation; the phone run produced the panel and PDF).
- Cross-lane pointers (not scored here): perf (overlay prefetch weight, load event at 41.6 s throttled), map-visual (F-19 mechanism, the legend's 0.3% note in F-01), Razor (F-06 proxy change, F-20 math, F-24 gate).
