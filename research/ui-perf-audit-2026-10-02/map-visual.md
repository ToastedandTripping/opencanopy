# OpenCanopy /map — visual + UI audit, 2026-10-01

Lane: visual + UI of opencanopy.ca/map (Jen). Production only, read-only on the repo (origin/main 3db5823 = live; bundle strings verified). Own headless Chromium, ~400 screenshots: the 08-22 like-for-like grid (11 configurations + baseline x 9 views x phone 390x844 DPR3 and desktop 1440x900) plus status, timeline, popup, panel, deep-link, search, share and colour probes. Evidence lives beside this file; every claim below cites a screenshot, a data file or file:line.

## Executive summary

1. The paint is byte-identical to 08-22 and the like-for-like coverage lands within a few points of it. The new problems are in the plumbing that decides whether that paint appears at all: four P0s, each an Effort-S fix.
2. **F-17 (P0):** until something happens to re-render the page, its map handle is `null`. On a fresh visit, search returns Tofino and does nothing. After the reader moves the map, Copy link shares `https://opencanopy.ca/map`. Camera-only links land 0/4.
3. **F-16 (P0):** on desktop at the province view MapLibre never fires `load`, so no vector layer loads until the reader touches the map (blank in 11/11 desktop configurations; phone unaffected, 10/10 loaded). This is the real cause of 08-22 P4/P5. The `[6,18]` zoomRange fix proposed in 08-22 would hide data that renders fine.
4. **F-02 (P0, regressed):** all six raster overviews sit above every label and data layer at z4-9 (live style order 50-55; first label 39). So Logging and Fire + Logging show forest age with a 15% tint of what the chip promises. This is the true cause of 08-22 PR1/PR3/K1 at those zooms, and the 07-09 label fix never took effect.
5. **F-18 (P0, the 09-01 refresh's R1-02, confirmed live):** the timeline opens at "1917 · 0 ha burned" over every fire up to 1950 (GPU global state 1950).
6. **Phone (P1):** the active preset is off-screen. The legend clips the classes that collide with fire. 17 of 24 visible controls are under 44 px and 13 are under 24 px. `100vh` on iOS (needs a device check). On desktop, the share button is clipped out of the bar.
7. **Honesty (P1):** popups still call VRI ≥250 stands "Old Growth", because the Batch 1 rename missed them. The share card claims "17 data layers" and species at risk. The Discover panel prints numbers that nothing derives, against the 2026-09-02 ruling. A first visit is an empty basemap.
8. **Colour (P1, still open since 07-15):** under protanopia, old growth vs harvested measures ΔE00 6.4. With both #0d5c2a and #ef4444 kept, no palette can do better than 7.3. A one-step lighter red (#f87171) with teal mature and yellow young lifts the minimum to 18.2. That palette is also the story's, so this is Lee's call.
9. **Prior items:** 8 fixed (P7, P8, K2, CP2, PR5, CH1-3), 3 renamed (PR2, PR4, the T2 copy) and 2 partly fixed (F2, S2). P1 and the 07-09 z-order fix regressed, and the 08-22 diagnoses for P4, P5 and PR3 are corrected here. Ship F-17, F-16, F-02 and F-18 before the Batch 2 paint re-spine, because at z≤9 any new paint would render under the raster.
10. **Method caveat:** the first third of the run used SwiftShader on a host at load average ~20, which starved MapLibre. I switched to the host's GPU, and every result that depended on the renderer was re-measured and is labelled. F-08 dropped from P0 to P1 as a result.

## Findings, ranked

Severity: P0 broken or lying to the public · P1 materially degrades the phone experience or trust · P2 clear improvement · P3 polish. Status: new / still-open / regressed / partially fixed. Full measurement detail for each ID is in Appendix A (chronological log, written as found).

### P0

**F-17 · P0 · NEW — The map page holds a null map handle until something re-renders it: search does nothing on a fresh visit, the share link ignores where the reader went, and camera-only links never land.**
- Evidence: `data/searchtest.jsonl` (search "Tofino", select → map stays z5 at -125.5, 54; the same steps after one preset tap → z12 at Tofino; `shots/search__desktop__fresh.png` vs `..._after-preset.png`). `data/copylinktest.jsonl` (fresh visit, wheel-zoom to z8.02, then Copy link → `https://opencanopy.ca/map`). Camera-only links 0/4 (`data/camera_repeat.jsonl`); `data/camtrace.jsonl` shows one map instance and no camera call.
- Cause: `src/components/map/CanopyMap.tsx:68` `useImperativeHandle(ref, () => mapRef.current!)` runs before react-map-gl creates the map and never again unless CanopyMap re-renders. Readers of that ref: search (`page.tsx:419-428`), deep-link fly and URL sync (`useMapState.ts:142-237`), share (`getShareUrl`), Select visible area, PDF export, timeline year sync and render gate.
- Fix: use a callback ref on `<Map>` that writes the outer ref the moment react-map-gl publishes the MapRef, and subscribe the URL-sync `moveend` once the map exists rather than on `enabledLayers`. Add a live guard spec (fresh context: search, select, assert zoom ≥ 10; a camera-only link must leave the scale bar off the z5 value). Effort S · confidence high.

**F-16 · P0 · STILL OPEN (08-22 P4; root cause corrected) — On desktop at the province view, no vector layer loads until the visitor touches the map.**
- Evidence: returning desktop visitor with Fire History saved: 3/3 runs, 45 s each, with no `load` mark, no PMTiles source and no fire layer. The phone viewport was fine 3/3 (`data/returning.jsonl`). Province view in the grid: desktop 11/11 configurations never requested the PMTiles header (160 raster 404s each); phone 10/10 did (6 404s). One `triggerRepaint()` → `load` → fire draws province-wide (`shots/z5load__desktop__before_after.png`).
- Cause: `PmtilesLayers` and `SatelliteLayers` register on MapLibre `load` (`DataLayer.tsx:422-426`, `:173-177`). `load` fires from `_render()` (`maplibre-gl-dev.js:73657-73661`), and a 404 schedules no repaint, so the map idles in a loaded state forever.
- Fix: register sources and layers on `style.load`, or at once when `getStyle()` is non-null, then `triggerRepaint()`. Do not ship 08-22's `zoomRange [6,18]` + "zoom in" chip. Guard: desktop live spec, `#lat=54&lng=-125.5&z=5&layers=fire-history` → a `.pmtiles` range request within 20 s, with no interaction. Effort S · confidence high.

**F-02 · P0 · REGRESSED (07-09 fix never in effect live) — At z4-9 the forest-age and old-growth rasters draw on top of every place name and every data layer.**
- Evidence: live style order (`data/mapinspect.jsonl`, read through a read-only React-hook handle on production): first symbol "Ocean labels" = 39; vector data 27-38; raster overviews **50-55**. Pixel proof on the Logging preset (`data/cutblock_attenuation.json`): at the cutblock-outline pixels, Logging is closer to forest-age-alone than to cutblocks-alone at z7/z9 (19.4 vs 37.1; 20.2 vs 42.4; 23.1 vs 33.7), and the relation flips at z11 (12.6 vs 62.7). Label glyphs are tinted by the raster: "Powell" over water renders rgb(184,184,184), while "River" over the raster renders rgb(141,194,160) (`shots/crop__labels_sheet__cta_desktop.png`), at the landing CTA's arrival view. On phone, 30 s after tapping Fire + Logging with the fire layer loaded, no perimeter is distinguishable (`shots/visitor__phone__fire-logging__1-after-chip-30s_1x.png`).
- Cause: `DataLayer.tsx:1086-1101` sets `rasterBeforeId` only when `isStyleLoaded()` is true. In MapLibre 5.21 that is false whenever a tile is pending, so `beforeId` stays undefined and the declarative raster `<Layer>`s append to the top.
- Fix: compute `beforeId` from `getStyle()?.layers` without the `isStyleLoaded()` guard (once at mount, on `load` and on `styledata`). Add a render test with `isStyleLoaded()` mocked false. This must ship before any Batch-2 paint work, or that work is invisible at z≤9. Effort S · confidence high.

**F-18 · P0 · STILL OPEN (09-01 refresh R1-02, confirmed live) — The timeline opens at "1917 · 0 ha burned" while the map shows every fire up to 1950.**
- Evidence: `data/yeartrace.jsonl`: on open, the control reads 1917, the filter is `FIRE_YEAR <= global-state.currentYear`, global state is `{currentYear: 1950}`, and no 1917 call was made (`setGlobalStateProperty` wrapped live). `shots/interact__timeline__phone__strip.png` frame 2 shows fire covering the screen under "0 ha burned through 1917".
- Fix: drop the `isStyleLoaded()` guard at `page.tsx:325`, set the year on `enable()`, and stop seeding 1950 in `CanopyMap.onLoad` (`:84`). Add a mock test for `calls.setGlobalState` (R4-16). Breaks CHARTER non-goal 3 today. Effort S · confidence high.

### P1

**F-13 · P1 · REGRESSED (08-22 P1, shipped in Batch 0) — Shared-link cameras miss 7 times in 25 fresh loads.** The misses cluster on fast first paints; the landing CTA landed in all 5 loads where its camera was checked (smoke x2, deeplinks, camera, throttled). Root cause is F-17: the layer-hydration re-render usually rescues the ref. Fix: seed `initialViewState` from the hash synchronously in CanopyMap, delete the poll-and-fly effect, and add a live spec (10 fresh loads at z9, scale never at the z5 value after 5 s). Evidence `data/camera_repeat.jsonl`, `camera.jsonl`, `deeplinks.jsonl`. Effort S.

**F-08 · P1 · NEW — A layer whose PMTiles source is not ready 15 s after `load` is abandoned for the session.** The legend stays lit and the map blank. A 6 s toast lists all six layers, including ones that are off, and blames "BC data". Reproduced 4/4 under starved rendering (`data/states.jsonl`, `shots/states__slow__phone__t100s_1x.png`); not reproduced on GPU at either the unthrottled or the Lighthouse-mobile profile (`data/timing.jsonl`, `throttled.jsonl`: `load` at 12.9-15.9 s, attached). Cause: `DataLayer.tsx:409-418` removes its listener on timeout and nothing re-arms it. Fix: never abandon. At 15 s set `loading` ("Still loading map data…"), keep listening, set `error` only on a real source error, and only for visible layers. Effort S.

Fix: the PMTiles path drives `loading`/`ok`; the badge shows inline in both legend states; `error` gets "Didn't load — tap to retry" plus a sticky toast that never names BC; T3 empty chip ("No 250+ yr stands in view"). Effort M.

## Method, coverage and limits

- **Target:** opencanopy.ca/map in production. The live JS chunks (`data/chunk_*.js`) carry the Batch 1 and 09-02 strings, so live = origin/main 3db5823. Nothing was edited, installed, committed, deployed or sent.
- **Browser:** this lane's own Playwright 1.58.2 headless Chromium (no MCP browsers), with a fresh context per capture. Viewports: phone 390x844 at DPR 3 with touch and the iPhone 14 user agent; desktop 1440x900 at DPR 1.
- **Renderer change mid-run (it matters):** captures before about 21:25 used SwiftShader. At a host load average of ~20 that starved MapLibre's render loop (MapLibre `load` at 41-52 s). From then on every capture uses ANGLE/EGL on the host's GTX 1060 (`scripts/lib.mjs:launch`, verified by `UNMASKED_RENDERER`). The grid, timeline, popups, panels, deep links, search, share and colour data are all GPU runs. The SwiftShader-era data is archived in `shots/grid-swiftshader/`, `grid-natural/` and `grid-pruned-abort/`, and is cited only where labelled (F-08's starved reproductions).

A camera-only baseline never lands (F-17); those first baselines are archived in `shots/grid-badbaseline/`. Coverage is the share of map pixels (chrome masked from DOM boxes recorded at capture) that differ from the baseline by more than 24/255 in any channel (`scripts/post.py`, `data/coverage_{desktop,phone}.json`). Cells at z11/z13 carry a few points of camera and label noise.
